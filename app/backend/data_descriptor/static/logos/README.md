# Third-party logos

The home page shows this logo in white, on the OMOP workflow's tile and in its navigation bar, to name the standard that
workflow works with. Small print on the tile says whose standard it is and that Flyover is not affiliated with its
owner. OHDSI does not endorse Flyover, sponsor it or have any affiliation with it. The RDF workflow's knowledge graph is
Flyover's own drawing, in [`../icons/`](../icons/).

## `ohdsi-logo-only-white.png`: the OHDSI logo, in white

- Owner: OHDSI (Observational Health Data Sciences and Informatics), which maintains the OMOP Common Data Model.
- Source: OHDSI's own white version, `OHDSI/OHDSI logo only - white.png` in <https://github.com/OHDSI/VisualIdentity>
  (retrieved 2026-11-09). Using a version OHDSI publishes keeps its design and colours intact: Flyover does not
  recolour, fade or crop it.
- Terms: the repository's "Policy Statement on the Use of Visual Identity for OHDSI" (effective 9 June 2023). It allows
  use for OHDSI-related activities, including digital media and related academic or research endeavours, as long as the
  design, colours and proportions stay intact and the use does not imply endorsement, sponsorship or affiliation with a
  product OHDSI has not recognised or approved. The policy names no licence, and the OHDSI Coordinating Center
  (contact@ohdsi.org) may review its use and change the policy without notice.
- The OMOP tile therefore says, beside the logo, that the OMOP CDM is maintained by OHDSI and that Flyover is not
  affiliated with OHDSI.
